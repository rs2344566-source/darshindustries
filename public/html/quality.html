QUALITY ASSURANCE

Committed To Excellence In Every Product

At Darsh Industries, quality is our highest priority. We manufacture precision-engineered railway components, rubber components and industrial products by following strict quality control procedures.

Every product passes through different stages of inspection to ensure accurate dimensions, proper material quality and reliable performance.

Our quality team uses advanced measuring instruments like Digital Vernier Caliper and Micrometer to check product size, thickness, diameter and critical dimensions as per required specifications.

OUR INSPECTION SYSTEM

Precision Quality Checking Process

Digital Vernier Caliper

Used for accurate measurement of product dimensions and size verification during manufacturing.

Micrometer Testing

Used for precise measurement of thickness, diameter and critical component dimensions.

Material Testing

Raw materials are checked to ensure strength, durability and quality before production.

Final Inspection

Final quality inspection is performed before dispatch to ensure customer requirements are achieved.
